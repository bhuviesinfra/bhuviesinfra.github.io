About us

Building, leasing and developing land across Hyderabad

Bhuvies Infra is a Hyderabad real-estate company led by Managing Director Smt. Sri Bhuvaneshwari Madthala. We build residential and commercial buildings, lease land to businesses, and procure land for our own projects and for other developers.

Our aim is simple: clear dealings, quality construction and spaces that fit the way people live and work.

  • Residential projects

    Apartments and standalone buildings, from planning to handover.

  • Commercial buildings

    Commercial spaces built for shops, offices and businesses.

  • Land on lease

    Land given on lease to businesses in growing parts of the city.

  • Land procurement and development

    Around 100 acres procured, developed by us or offered to developers.
